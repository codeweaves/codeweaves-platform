## 1. Subject matter and duration

1.1 This DPA governs the Processor's processing of personal data on behalf of the Fiduciary in
connection with the Klivo AI-agent platform, for the duration of the Agreement.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

2.1 Operating AI chat/voice agents on the Fiduciary's digital properties and messaging channels;
generating automated responses; enabling human takeover of conversations; capturing contact/lead
details End Users choose to provide; providing the Fiduciary with conversation history and
aggregated analytics.

## 3. Categories of data and data principals

3.1 **Data principals:** the Fiduciary's website visitors, customers and prospects ("End Users").
3.2 **Data categories:** conversation content (text and voice transcriptions); contact and lead
details (e.g. name, email, phone, custom fields configured by the Fiduciary); technical
identifiers (device identifier, hashed IP address, WhatsApp phone number where applicable);
operational logs.

## 4. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:
(a) process personal data only on documented instructions of the Fiduciary, including as
    configured by the Fiduciary in the platform;
(b) implement reasonable security safeguards, including encryption of sensitive stored data,
    encryption in transit, tenant isolation, role-based access control and audit logging;
(c) ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality obligations;
(d) assist the Fiduciary in responding to data-principal requests (access, correction, erasure)
    through the platform's built-in mechanisms, within [PLACEHOLDER — ASSIST_SLA, e.g. 10 business
    days];
(e) notify the Fiduciary **without undue delay** and no later than [PLACEHOLDER — BREACH_NOTICE,
    e.g. 48 hours] after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Fiduciary's data,
    providing information reasonably required for the Fiduciary's own notification duties;
(f) delete the Fiduciary's personal data upon termination of the Agreement or on written request,
    within 30 days, except where retention is required by law;
(g) make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.
